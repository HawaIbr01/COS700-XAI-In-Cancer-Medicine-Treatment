This study adopts a \textit{proof by demonstration} research methodology (NICOGSlides).
The hypothesis is evaluated through a controlled experiment in which the optimisation strategy
(Adam baseline versus SAM intervention) serves as the independent variable, and rule fidelity,
comprehensibility, and stability serve as dependent variables. All other components of the
pipeline — the DeepCCDS architecture, the GDSC dataset, the data splits, the EA configuration,
and the evaluation framework — are held constant across both experimental conditions. This
controlled substitution isolates the contribution of loss landscape geometry to explanation quality
and ensures that observed differences in rule quality can be attributed specifically to the
regularisation intervention rather than to confounding factors.
