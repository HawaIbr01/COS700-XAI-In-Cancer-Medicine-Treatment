\label{sec:planning}

The project is structured across four months from July to October 2026. All five team members
begin with shared boilerplate and pilot testing in July to establish a common codebase and verify
the pipeline end-to-end. Data preparation and baseline model training are completed in July as well.
Rule extraction by Persons 2 and 3, alongside the SAM intervention by Person 4, run in
parallel across August and September. Person 5 conducts PROMETHEE II ranking, statistical
validation, and biological verification on a rolling basis from August through October as rule
sets are produced. Final evaluation, comparative analysis, and report writing are completed
collaboratively by all team members, wrapping up with final submissions in October.
