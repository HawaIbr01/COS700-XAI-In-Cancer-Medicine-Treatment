While  standard Genetic Algorithms usually combine multiple goals into a single scalar fitness function,the Non-Dominated Sorting 
Genetic Algorithm II(NSGA II) is explicitly designed for multi-objective optimisation tasks where objectives are fundamentally in 
conflict. In our context of rule extraction ,achieving high fidelity ,achieving high fidelity to the underlying Artificial Neural 
Network often comes at the expense of comprehensibility.
\\\\
NSGA II addresses  this trade-off by evolving a population 	of solutions toward a Pareto front which is a set of optimal solutions where 
no single objective can be improved  without reducing the other.The  algorithm operates on two core mechanisms :
\\
1)Fast Non-Dominated Sorting:  The population is ranked into hierarchical fronts  based on pareto dominance .An individual rule set is 
considered “non-dominated” if no other rule set in the population is superior across all objectives.
\\
2)Crowding Distance: To maintain diversity within the population and prevent premature convergence on a local optimum,NSGA II 
calculates a crowding distance for each individual.This metric estimates the  density of solutions surrounding a particular rule 
set in the objective space ,prioritising individuals residing in less crowded regions during the selection phase
\\
By utilising elitism ,which means carrying the best solutions from the combined parents and offspring populations into the next 
generation ,NSGA II ensures that high quality rule sets are not lost during evolution. For this RED XAI framework ,NSGA  II is 
highly advantageous as it naturally outputs a diverse spectrum of rule sets ,allowing the PROMETHEE II multi criteria  framework 
to later rank and select the optimal balance between mitigating “Rule Explosion” and maintaining statistical fidelity.
